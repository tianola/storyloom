# Jensen Huang Research & Facts

## Core Narrative Arc
1. **The Hook (0:00 - 0:30)**: In 1993, at a Denny's diner in San Jose, three engineers sketched out a company that would build chips to render 3D polygons in real-time. Thirty years later, that same company became the single engine of the artificial intelligence revolution, crossing trillions in valuation. But the man behind the leather jacket didn't start with AI—he started with failure, near-bankruptcy, and a bet on a bizarre mathematical paradigm called parallel computing.
2. **Origins & Early Struggles (0:30 - 2:00)**: Born in Taiwan, moved to Thailand, then sent to a rugged Baptist boarding school in Kentucky cleaning toilets. Moving to Oregon State to study electrical engineering. Founding NVIDIA with Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem. The near-death experience of NV1 vs. Microsoft DirectX, and the pivotal pivot to the RIVA 128 and GeForce 256.
3. **The CUDA Bet (2:00 - 4:30)**: Around 2006, Jensen made a multi-billion dollar bet against Wall Street: adding C-language programmability to GPUs (CUDA). For years, analysts hammered NVIDIA stock because CUDA compressed profit margins for speculative scientific computing. But it turned GPUs from graphics cards into general-purpose parallel supercomputers.
4. **The AI Explosion & Architecture (4:30 - 6:30)**: How AlexNet in 2012 used CUDA GPUs to shatter ImageNet benchmarks, sparking the deep learning boom. The scaling laws: from DGX-1 to H100 and Blackwell. The hardware anatomy of modern AI accelerators—high-bandwidth memory (HBM), tensor cores, NVLink interconnects.
5. **Personal Life & Recent Revelations (6:30 - 8:00)**: The iconic leather jacket, management style (flat hierarchy with 50 direct reports), resilience through multiple near-death market shifts. Closing on the recent personal headlines regarding his marriage and personal life developments, contrasting the public titan of silicon with the human reality behind the relentless hardware empire.

## Key Facts & Figures
- Founded: April 5, 1993.
- Initial funding: $40,000.
- Pivotal moment: 1997 RIVA 128 saved NVIDIA when they were 30 days from payroll exhaustion.
- CUDA release: 2006.
- Market cap milestone: Crossed $1 trillion in 2023, surging past $3 trillion in 2024.
