# World bible (pilot v3)

Rules: .claude/skills/story-film/references/staging.md §5-7. Each place below must be visible in its establishing shot.

## Places

### Asylum, Vienna, summer night 1865 (beat 1-2)
- **Use:** confinement. **People at task:** two guards bringing a man in; a gatekeeper with a lantern.
- **Period markers:** oil lantern on an iron bracket, studded wooden gate, cobbles, guards' uniforms.
- **Cell:** straw on the floor, a bucket, a stool with a tin bowl, a chain ring in the wall, a barred window with the
  moon; the shaft of moonlight falls across the straw where he sits. **Wear:** damp stains, scratched plaster.
- **Light/colour:** cold blue moon against one warm lantern; night key-to-fill 6-8:1. The coldest scene of the film.

### Vienna rooftops and streets, 1846 (beat 3-4)
- **Use:** a working town at the end of the day. **People at task:** market sellers packing up, buyers, a handcart
  pushed home, neighbours talking at doors, the lamplighter on his round, washing taken in.
- **Period markers:** oil street lamps lit by hand, shop signs and awnings, carts, chimney smoke, dress of the 1840s.
- **Windows:** rooms with lamps, curtains, shelves; silhouettes crossing; some go dark later.
- **The narrator's spot:** the explorer always speaks from the same rooftop corner, screen right.
- **Colour:** warm dusk to blue night as the descent goes down; warm windows against the cooling sky.

### Semmelweis's room behind the hospital window, night 1846 (beat 4-5)
- **Use:** work late. **Personal objects:** books on a shelf, papers and the clinic register on the desk, an ink
  well, a candle and an oil lamp, a narrow bed, a picture on the wall. **Wear:** papers piled, a chair pushed back.
- **Light:** the oil lamp is the key; the window is the only other light. Push-in on his face for the thought.

### The clinic court, dawn 1846 (beat 6)
- **Use:** the doors of the two clinics. **People at task:** women queueing at the Second Clinic with a midwife;
  students talking at the First; orderlies carrying a covered stretcher; a midwife crossing.
- **Detail:** the two doors under their lamps, cobbles, a pump, benches, trees, a parked cart.
- **Colour:** grey-blue dawn, warm door lamps still lit.

### The First Clinic ward, day 1846 (beat 7, 9)
- **Use:** births and the dying. **People at task:** women in iron beds; a midwife moving between beds; a doctor and
  students examining at one bed; a woman in labour helped; the priest crossing with the acolyte ringing his bell;
  Semmelweis at the door by the register.
- **Detail:** two rows of beds with straw mattresses and grey blankets, night tables with tin cups, a crucifix, a wash
  stand with a basin and towel (no chlorine yet), a linen cupboard, the register and an oil lamp by the door.
- **Colour:** cool daylight from tall windows; the bell scene is the only warm-gold moment (the priest's stole, the
  lamp by the door).

### The dissecting room, morning 1847 (beat 8-9)
- **Use:** autopsies and teaching. **People at task:** Kolletschka lecturing at the table, students around it, two
  late students coming in, jars on shelves, a basin.
- **Colour:** sickly warm lamp over the table, cold walls.
