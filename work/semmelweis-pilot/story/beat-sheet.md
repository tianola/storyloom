# Beat sheet (pilot v3, written with .claude/skills/story-film)

**Spine:** Semmelweis wants to stop the mothers dying in his clinic, **but** nobody knows why they die there and not
next door, **therefore** he tests every explanation of his day, one by one; it will cost him his career and his mind.
**Kind:** discovery against resistance (topics.md 1). **Opening:** stakes before names, at the asylum (script.md §2.1).
**Pilot ends** on the question; the payoff (chlorine washing, May 1847) belongs to the full film.
